LEARN IN FIVE SMALL STEPS

Play a round from start to finish

Follow the numbers ↓
01

Choose your crew

Pick 1–4 players, enter names, choose your characters, select a realm, and choose a 50-, 75-, or 100-tile race. You can also add a computer opponent.

✦ Each hero has an active power you choose to use and a passive ability that activates automatically.

02

Spin the colorful wheel

On your turn, tap SPIN WHEEL. The number under the pointer tells you how far to move. Follow the numbered path across the board.

✦ Example: Spin 4 → move four tiles (unless a power changes your movement).

03

Check where you land

A normal tile is safe: no question! A tile with a glowing portal icon starts a math encounter. The portal’s outcome is different on each visit.

✦ Look for the bright blue-purple portal icons on the board.

04

Flip the portal mystery card

Tap the face-down mystery card to reveal Ladder, Dragon’s Lair, or Chest of Faith. Then choose the answer to the math question. You can open your Math Notebook to work it out.

✦ Only portal encounters give math questions.

05

See the result and pass the turn

Correct answers earn the portal’s good outcome or let you escape the dragon. Incorrect answers follow that portal’s penalty. A short countdown then starts the next player’s turn.

✦ Keep taking turns until someone reaches the final tile with an exact spin.

THE MAGIC PORTALS

Three possible surprises

Each time you enter a portal, you reveal one of these three outcomes. The same portal can give a different outcome on another visit.

🪜↗

Ladder

Correct: Climb forward to the tile shown.

Wrong: Stay on the portal tile.

🐉🛡

Dragon's Lair

Correct: Escape the attack. Lose no tiles!

Wrong: Move backward unless protected by a shield or ability.

🎁✦

Chest of Faith

Correct: Open the chest to receive a random reward.

Wrong: No chest reward this visit.

REWARDS YOU CAN USE

What is inside Chest of Faith?

Examples of the animated tools you can place or use in the game
🛡️
Ank Shield

Blocks a dragon setback or opponent attack; carry up to 2.

🔮
Mana Power

Reduces the cooldown of your active power by 1 turn.

🪤
Bear Trap

Choose the tool, then tap a board tile. An opponent crossing it loses their next spin.

💥
Air Strike

Push each other player in your current 10-tile row back 1 tile.

❄️
Snowball

Reduce a chosen player’s next wheel movement by 2 (minimum 1).

🧪
Lava Flask

Tap a non-portal tile to place lava. Players crossing it lose their next spin.

👆
Placing a Bear Trap or Lava Puddle: Open Chest of Faith, select the tool, and tap a highlighted valid tile on the board. Tap Cancel to keep your tool. Chest rewards are separate from character powers.

Active power

You choose when to use it before spinning. After use, wait the listed number of your own turns before it is ready again.

✦
Passive ability

Works automatically when its condition happens. Some have a recharge; others are always active.

THE FINAL CHALLENGE

How do you win?

98→99→🏆100

Land exactly on the finish tile.

If you have 2 tiles left, you need exactly 2 movement to win. A spin that goes past the finish does not end the race. Keep playing until you land exactly on the final tile.

✦ The finish is Tile 50, 75, or 100, depending on the race you selected.
